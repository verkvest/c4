# Third-party licenses

Aureline ships the game engine from c4 and a Capacitor native shell. No third-party fonts, music, photographs, or artwork are bundled. The app icon and in-app mark are original and generated by `scripts/render-brand-assets.py`. Sound is synthesized in the browser. The mobile build does not contact a match server.
